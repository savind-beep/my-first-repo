Welcome to Sri Lanka!


ABOUT

Sri Lanka is a beautiful island nation located in the Indian Ocean. It is known for its rich culture, diverse wildlife, and stunning landscapes.


HISTORY

Sri Lanka has a long and fascinating history that dates back over 2,500 years. It was ruled by various kingdoms and empires, including the Chola, Pandya, and British Empires. The country gained independencein 1948 and has since developed into a modern nation with a thriving economy.


CIVILIZATION

Sri Lanka is home to a rich and diverse civilization that has been shaped by its geography, history, and culture. The country has a unique blend of South Asian and Southeast Asian influences, which is reflected in its art, architecture, and cuisine.
