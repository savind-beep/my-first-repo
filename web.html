NSBM green university

NSBM Green University is the first ever green university in South Asia and sets an example for the whole South Asia by paving the way for environmental sustainability. Officially established in 2011, our university has been committed to providing high-quality, globally recognized education to meet the growing needs of Sri Lanka’s higher education sector. In 2016, our journey reached a significant milestone with the opening of our state-of-the-art university town in Homagama, located in Colombo Suburbs, which marked a new era for Sri Lankan education. This purpose-built campus is designed to offer a modern, student-friendly environment that supports academic excellence, innovation, and sustainable living.
